Kitsune is an open-source, modern, lightweight, and completely private media downloader for Android. It operates 100% on-device, extracting and downloading audio and video locally without routing your data through external intermediary servers.

Features:
- Processed 100% locally on-device using bundled yt-dlp and FFmpeg binaries
- Multi-platform support: videos and audio tracks from YouTube, TikTok, public Instagram Reels, X (Twitter), Reddit, Bilibili, SoundCloud, Pinterest, and direct media links
- Download queue with sequential background processing and pending indicators
- Multi-item playlist extraction with selection dialog and duration calculation
- Wi-Fi only mode for data-saving operation
- Dynamic Theming (Material You) on Android 12+ and pure AMOLED dark theme
- Built-in media player powered by Media3 ExoPlayer for videos and thematic animations for audio
- In-app media library for playback, renaming, sharing, and deleting files
- Automatic stripping of URL tracking parameters (utm, si, igsh, fbclid)
- Full internationalization with English and Brazilian Portuguese support
- 100% open-source software licensed under the GPLv3 license
- Zero tracking, zero telemetry, and zero ads
